How this was counted

  • Source: the Foundry VTT chat exports in SessionLogs/, one file per session. Only rolls that reached chat are counted; GM rolls kept fully private are not.
  • "Natural" is the d20 face before modifiers. Attack rolls, saves, skill checks and flat checks all count.
  • Secret party checks (Perception, Religion, Society, Diplomacy) are rolled by the GM's button but credited to each character. Use the toggle to leave them out.
  • The GM entry is every monster, NPC and hazard plus the Gamemaster login. "Unassigned" is the shared player login and Tomas's login, whose character the log can't tell.
  • Damage rolled is the total of damage rolls posted by that roller (healing excluded). Damage taken comes from Foundry's "takes N damage" messages; for the GM it means damage the party dealt to monsters.
